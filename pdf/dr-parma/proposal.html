01 Overview

Two AI agents built exclusively for Dr. Parma's clinic. Agent 1 (LeadHunter) finds patients actively searching for a family physician across Reddit, Twitter, Nextdoor, Facebook, Yelp, Google Maps and healthcare review platforms — scores intent and surfaces ranked lead cards for Dr. Parma's approval. Agent 2 (ReputationGuard) monitors all review platforms 24/7, detects negative reviews, drafts HIPAA-safe responses, and posts on approval — protecting Dr. Parma's online reputation in real time.

02 Agent 1 — LeadHunter

Target: people searching for a PCP, family doctor or GP within Dr. Parma's service radius.

Sources Monitored
  • Reddit — r/AskDocs, r/HealthInsurance, r/medical
  • Twitter/X — keyword & location triggers
  • Facebook Groups — local community groups
  • Nextdoor — neighbor recommendations
  • Yelp Health, Google Maps Q&A
  • Healthgrades, RateMDs, Vitals
  • Zocdoc patient search activity

03 Agent 2 — ReputationGuard
Platforms Monitored
  • Google Business Profile
  • Yelp · Healthgrades
  • RateMDs · Vitals
  • Facebook Reviews · Zocdoc
Process
  1. Polls all platforms every 6 hours for new reviews
  2. Sentiment analysis — Positive / Neutral / Negative
  3. Categorises issue — wait, staff, billing, medical, availability
  4. AI drafts HIPAA-safe, legally cautious reply
  5. Dr. Parma reviews — Edit · Approve & Post · Discard
  6. Agent posts approved reply on the same thread
Example AI Reply

"Hi [Name], thank you for sharing your experience. Long wait times and anything less than a warm welcome is not what we stand for. Please call us at [phone] and ask for our patient care coordinator — we'd love to make this right. Your feedback helps us improve. We hope to see you again. — Dr. Parma's Team"
