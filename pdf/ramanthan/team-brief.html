04 Risks & Mitigations
⚠ Risk

Ramanthan uses analogue phones — standard VoIP tap won't work

✓ Mitigation

Use hardware audio tap on the phone line; feed audio into the pipeline directly

⚠ Risk

Tamil/medical accent causing low transcription accuracy (>15% WER)

✓ Mitigation

Fine-tune Whisper on Tamil-English medical vocabulary; use Large model if medium falls short

⚠ Risk

High simultaneous call volume overwhelming processing queue

✓ Mitigation

Implement queue system so calls process sequentially without data loss; prioritise by doctor
