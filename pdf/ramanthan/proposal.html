01 Overview

A private, secure, on-premise system that automatically records every doctor-patient consultation call, transcribes it in real time, and stores everything locally on your own server. No data ever leaves your premises. No external API. No per-minute charges. Ever.

🔒 100% Local Processing — No Audio Leaves Your Server
02 What We Build
  • Records every doctor-patient phone/consultation call automatically
  • Transcribes the full call to clean text format in real time
  • Stores all transcripts locally — 100% private, no data leaves your server
  • No external API used — all processing done on your own machine (open-source Whisper model)
  • Speaker diarization — Doctor and Patient voices are labelled separately (Tier B)
  • Each transcript tagged: Date, Doctor Name, Patient ID, Duration
  • Search any transcript by keyword, date, or doctor name
  • Export as .txt or .pdf
Why no external API?
Your patients' medical conversations are private. We use open-source local models (Whisper by OpenAI, run entirely on your server) so no audio ever leaves your premises. HIPAA-safe by design.
