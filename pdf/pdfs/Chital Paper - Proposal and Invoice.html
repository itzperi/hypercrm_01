01 The Decision Framework
The right platform depends on one number: how many customers visit your site per day.
Under 100 visitors/day → Custom Coded Website. Over 100 visitors/day → Shopify.
Option 1 — Custom Coded
Best if < 100 visitors / day
Our Pick
✓One-time build — yours forever, no monthly platform fee
✓Full control over design, features, integrations
✓Adding new features later: cost-effective (pay once per feature)
✗Each new feature requires a developer (cheaper long-term than Shopify)

Pricing by product count:

13–15 products₹50,000
Up to 30 products₹70,000
Up to 50 products₹1,00,000
+ Server: ₹10,000/month · Domain: ₹800–₹1,500/yr (if new)
Option 2 — Shopify
Best if > 100 visitors / day
✓Built for high traffic, scales automatically
✓Built-in payment gateway, inventory, analytics
✓Fast to launch — no custom dev for standard features
✗Monthly platform fee: ₹1,500–₹5,000/month
✗Transaction fees: 0.5%–2% on every order
✗Shopify apps: ₹500–₹3,000/month each — adds up fast
✗Cannot be quoted exactly upfront — depends on apps chosen
Domain: ₹800–₹1,500/yr (if new). Shopify plan fees paid directly to Shopify.
Our Recommendation for Chital Paper:
If you're launching fresh and expect under 100 visitors/day initially, the custom coded website gives you the best value — you own it completely, no recurring platform fees, and every rupee goes into your business. Start with ₹50,000 for 13–15 products and scale up as your catalogue grows.
