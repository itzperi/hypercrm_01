01 Overview

Hyperwrike has completed 5+ dedicated voice tuning sessions for VS Hospitals. The AI voice model is finalised — trained on VS Hospitals' specialties, doctors, packages, and Chennai patient demographics including Tanglish. It is deployed on a GPU-enabled dedicated server hosted solely for VS Hospitals.

This proposal covers one branch only, end-to-end: inbound and outbound voice, chat across Website and WhatsApp, CRM and telephony integration, and a live ROI & usage portal — all maintained under a single per-minute rate with no monthly fee.

02 Why ₹1,50,000
  • 5+ voice tuning sessions — model finalised, not in development
  • GPU-enabled dedicated server hosted solely for VS Hospitals
  • Zoho CRM + Ozonetel — two full enterprise integrations
  • Chatbot vendor coordination — Website + WhatsApp
  • ROI Dashboard + Usage Minutes Portal — built and maintained
  • 30+ specialties, doctor list & 5 campaigns in knowledge base
  • Tanglish fine-tuned for the Chennai patient base
  • All hosting, monitoring & KB updates inside ₹6/min — no monthly fee
