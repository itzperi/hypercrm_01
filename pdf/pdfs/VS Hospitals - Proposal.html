01 Overview

This proposal covers the development and deployment of an AI Voice Agent across all three VS Hospitals branches — Tondiarpet, Kilpauk and Chetpet — under a single unified engagement. The AI voice model is already finalised after 5+ dedicated tuning sessions, trained on VS Hospitals' specialties, doctors, packages, and Chennai patient demographics including Tanglish.

Tondiarpet is the primary focus branch and receives the full AI Voice Agent with complete patient-handling capabilities. Kilpauk and Chetpet receive a campaign-focused AI Voice Agent for promotional and awareness activities.

03 Branch-Wise Scope
Tondiarpet — Primary Focus Branch
Full AI Voice Agent with complete patient-handling capabilities:
  • Inbound patient handling — appointment booking, doctor availability, department routing
  • Outbound follow-up — CRM-triggered calls, lead follow-up, dead-lead revival
  • Pre-appointment reminder calls — automated, 1 hour before slot
  • FAQ handling — visiting hours, insurance accepted, directions, parking
  • Campaign communication — health packages, offers, health checkup & awareness campaigns
  • Chat System — Website, WhatsApp, click-to-call
  • Zoho CRM Integration — leads, notes, follow-ups, triggers
  • Knowledge Base — 30+ specialties, doctors, packages, 5 campaigns
  • English + Tanglish language support
Kilpauk & Chetpet — Campaign-Focused
AI Voice Agent limited to campaign-related communication:
  • Health packages & offers — informing patients of current packages and discounts
  • Health checkup campaigns — master health check, annual checkup, seasonal screenings
  • Awareness campaigns — diabetes, cardiac, women's health, vaccination drives
  • Promotional activities — new doctor onboarding announcements, new department launches
  • Outbound campaign calls — targeted call lists for specific promotions
