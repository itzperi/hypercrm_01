We are pleased to present this proposal for the development and deployment of an AI-Powered Patient Communication & CRM System for VS Hospitals. This engagement covers Tondiarpet (primary) and Kilpauk & Chetpet (campaign-focused) branches under a single unified development scope.

Following receipt of the advance, work commences immediately. The planning & roadmap document is delivered within 24 hours of advance receipt.

02 Scope of Work
  • Inbound patient handling — appointment booking, doctor availability, department routing
  • Outbound follow-up — CRM-triggered calls, lead follow-up, dead-lead revival
  • Pre-appointment reminder calls — automated, 1 hour before slot
  • FAQ handling — visiting hours, insurance, directions, parking
  • Campaign communication — health packages, offers, awareness campaigns
  • Chat System — Website, WhatsApp, click-to-call
  • Zoho CRM Integration — leads, notes, follow-ups, triggers
  • Knowledge Base — 30+ specialties, doctors, packages, 5 campaigns
  • English + Tanglish language support
  • GPU-enabled server procurement & setup
  • Telephony via Vobiz with VS Hospitals branding
  • MCP integration with Zoho CRM
