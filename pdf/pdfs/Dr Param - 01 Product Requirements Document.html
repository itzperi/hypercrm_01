01 Executive Summary

Dr. Param is building a direct-to-consumer telemedicine platform modelled on Sesame, Zocdoc, and Klarity, with the clinical and operational backbone of an "open-loop" provider network similar to OpenLoop Health. The platform lets a patient discover a service, complete a guided intake, get matched with a licensed clinician, receive care by video or asynchronous review, and leave with a prescription, lab order, or care plan — while a separate clinician workspace and an internal operations console run everything behind the scenes.

The platform is three connected products — the Patient App, the Provider Dashboard, and the Admin Console — released across three versions so the client can launch fast, prove the model with real patients and providers, and only invest in heavier infrastructure (insurance billing, controlled-substance prescribing, multi-brand white-label) once V1 has validated demand.

02 Product Vision & Goals
  • Let a patient go from "I need care" to "a licensed clinician has seen my case" in under 10 minutes for async visits, or one scheduled video call for sync visits.
  • Give clinicians a single queue-driven workspace to review intakes, run visits, document care, and prescribe — without juggling separate EHR, eRx, and video tools.
  • Give Dr. Param's operations team full visibility and control: provider supply, compliance status, complaints, and revenue, in one console.
  • Build on a HIPAA-eligible technical foundation from day one so V2/V3 features can be added without re-architecting.
  • Ship a real, prescribable, billable V1 in approximately 17 weeks for a fixed investment of $44,000.
03 Target Users & Personas
Patient
Adult self-pay (and later insured) consumer looking for fast, convenient access to a specific service (weight loss, mental health, primary care).
Need: Be seen quickly by a real, licensed clinician without visiting a clinic.
Clinician / Provider
Licensed NP, PA, or physician contracted or employed by the platform, often working across multiple states.
Need: Review cases and run visits efficiently inside one workspace; get paid correctly and on time.
Platform Admin / Ops
Dr. Param's internal team — operations, compliance, and support staff.
Need: See everything happening on the platform and intervene when something needs attention.

05 Technical Architecture Principles
  • HIPAA-eligible from day one — every PHI-touching service runs behind a signed BAA; retrofitting compliance later is far more expensive.
  • Data-driven configuration — service lines, intake questionnaires, and state coverage are configured, not hard-coded, so adding a new condition or state in V2/V3 doesn't require new code.
  • Vendor stack (V1) — Stripe (payments), DoseSpot or Doxy.me (eRx), video vendor TBD at discovery, Twilio or AWS Transcribe (comms), AWS/GCP with HIPAA BAA (hosting).
  • Shared data model — Patient App, Provider Dashboard, and Admin Console all share one underlying patient/provider/visit data model, built once, used three ways.
