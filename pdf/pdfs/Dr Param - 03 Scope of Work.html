01 Purpose

This Scope of Work is issued under, and incorporated into, the Master Services Agreement between Hyperwrike and Dr. Param. It governs the delivery of Version 1 of the telemedicine platform as described in the Product Requirements Document v1.0 (HW-PRD-PRM-2026-001). Where this SOW conflicts with the PRD on feature scope, this SOW controls; where it conflicts with the MSA on legal terms, the MSA controls.

03 Out of Scope (V1)
  • Insurance eligibility checks, claims submission, or payer billing
  • Asynchronous (store-and-forward) visits
  • Secure in-app messaging beyond visit-bound notes
  • Native iOS/Android applications (V1 = responsive web app)
  • Controlled-substance e-prescribing / EPCS workflow
  • Automated background-check or credentialing integrations
  • EHR interoperability, SOC 2 audit support, white-label/multi-brand
  • Recruiting, contracting, payroll, or malpractice insurance for clinicians
  • Paid marketing, content writing, or SEO beyond structural landing page
