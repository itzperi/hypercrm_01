WHEREAS, Client wishes to engage Service Provider to design, develop, and deliver a telemedicine software platform, and Service Provider wishes to provide such services, on the terms set out below and in each Scope of Work ("SOW") executed under this Agreement.

Article 1 — Definitions
1.1"Services" means the software design, development, testing, deployment, and related professional services described in any SOW executed under this Agreement.
1.2"Deliverables" means the source code, documentation, designs, and other work product Service Provider produces specifically for Client under an SOW.
1.3"SOW" means a Scope of Work document referencing this Agreement, describing specific deliverables, timeline, and fees, signed by both Parties.
1.4"Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by either Party that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.
1.5"PHI" means Protected Health Information as defined under HIPAA and its implementing regulations.
1.6"BAA" means a Business Associate Agreement as required under HIPAA, to be executed separately as Exhibit B if Service Provider's role requires access to PHI.
Article 2 — Services & Scope of Work
2.1Service Provider will perform the Services described in each SOW. Each SOW, once signed by both Parties, is incorporated into and governed by this Agreement.
2.2In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and an SOW, this Agreement controls on legal/commercial terms and the SOW controls on technical scope and feature detail.
2.3Either Party may propose changes to an active SOW only through the written change-request process defined in that SOW.

Article 3 — Term & Termination
3.1This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until all active SOWs are completed or terminated.
3.2Either Party may terminate an SOW for material breach if the breaching Party fails to cure within 15 days of written notice.
3.3Client may terminate an SOW for convenience on 15 days' written notice; Client pays for all Services performed and non-cancellable costs committed through the effective date of termination.
3.4Upon termination, Service Provider delivers all completed and in-progress Deliverables for which payment has been made. Confidentiality, IP, and payment obligations survive termination.
Article 4 — Fees, Invoicing & Taxes
4.1Fees for each SOW are as set out in that SOW's payment schedule. Unless otherwise stated, fees are quoted exclusive of applicable taxes.
4.2Invoices are payable within 5 business days of issuance. Amounts unpaid more than 15 days past due may accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum permitted by law, whichever is lower. Service Provider may suspend Services until payment is current.
4.3If Services qualify as a zero-rated export of services, Service Provider will issue invoices accordingly upon Client furnishing required documentation. Each Party should confirm the correct tax treatment with its own advisors.
4.4Client is responsible for all third-party/vendor costs named in the applicable SOW (hosting, video, eRx, payment processing) billed directly by those vendors.
Article 5 — Intellectual Property
5.1Upon full payment of all fees due for an SOW, Service Provider assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in the Deliverables created specifically for Client under that SOW, excluding Background IP.
5.2"Background IP" means tools, libraries, frameworks, templates, and know-how owned by Service Provider prior to the engagement. Service Provider grants Client a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use Background IP embedded in the Deliverables solely as part of the Deliverables.
5.3Client retains ownership of all data it provides, including patient and provider data generated through use of the platform. Service Provider claims no ownership over Client's business data.
Article 6 — Confidentiality
6.1Each Party will protect the other's Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and not less than reasonable care.
6.2These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was already known to the receiving Party, or is required to be disclosed by law.
Article 7 — Governing Law & Disputes
7.1This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, USA. The Parties consent to jurisdiction in Delaware courts for any dispute not resolved by the process in §7.2.
7.2The Parties will attempt to resolve any dispute by good-faith negotiation for 30 days before initiating arbitration or litigation.
