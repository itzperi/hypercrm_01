Article Verification & Publishing Platform
01 Overview

A public-facing web platform where users submit news articles. Each submission is automatically reviewed by Hyperwrike's AI Verification Agent — which scores the article across 6 weighted dimensions before allowing or rejecting publication. No human editor bottleneck. No bad-faith content gets through.

02 How It Works
  1. A user visits the website and submits an article.
  2. The AI Verification Agent runs a 6-metric analysis.
  3. Score computed — above threshold: auto-approved and published; below threshold: rejected with detailed feedback.
  4. Edge cases are flagged for an optional human review queue.
03 AI Verification Metrics
DimensionWeightWhat it checks
Factual Accuracy30%Plausible stats? Named sources? Impossible claims flagged?
Bias Score20%Neutral language? Partisan framing? Both sides represented?
Source Quality20%Named experts? Institutions cited? Anonymous sources flagged?
Writing Clarity10%Grammar, structure, readability score
Originality10%Clickbait signals, duplicate-content markers, specificity of detail
Policy Compliance10%Hate speech, medical misinformation, defamation risk, legal red flags

Two AI agents built exclusively for Dr. Parma's clinic. Agent 1 (LeadHunter) finds patients actively searching for a family physician across Reddit, Twitter, Nextdoor, Facebook, Yelp, Google Maps and healthcare review platforms — scores intent and surfaces ranked lead cards for Dr. Parma's approval. 02 Agent 1 — LeadHunter

Target: people searching for a PCP, family doctor or GP within Dr. Parma's service radius.

Home Services Marketplace
01 Overview

Two AI agents that run 24/7 without human intervention, building both sides of the FixDirectly marketplace. Agent 1 finds homeowners who need home services and surfaces them as qualified leads. Agent 2 finds service providers in the same area, calls them via an AI voice agent, and converts them into FixDirectly sign-ups — completely automated, around the clock.

02 Agent 1 — Customer Lead Scraper
Sources
  • Yelp — "need a plumber", "HVAC not working", "roof repair"
  • Nextdoor — neighbor service requests
  • Facebook Marketplace & community groups
  • Craigslist service requests
  • Google Maps Q&A — competitor service pages
  • HomeAdvisor, Angi, Thumbtack public listings
Method
  • Keyword + location trigger detection
  • Real-time scraping of public posts & listings
  • Lead card — Name/Handle, Platform, Service Needed, Location, Post URL, Urgency Score

Output delivered to the FixDirectly dashboard — sorted by urgency and service category.

03 Agent 2 — Service Provider Recruiter · AI Voice · 24/7
Finds
  • Independent contractors & small home-service businesses on Yelp, Google Maps, Thumbtack, Angi, HomeAdvisor
  • Categories — plumbing, electrical, HVAC, roofing, cleaning, pest control, landscaping, painting
Process
  1. AI scrapes provider listings & extracts phone numbers
  2. AI Voice Agent calls the provider
  3. Delivers the recruitment script
  4. Interested providers land on the signup page
  5. Call logged — interested / not / voicemail
Voice Script

"Hi, this is an automated message from FixDirectly — a platform that sends you paying jobs in your area. If you're interested in getting more customers with no upfront cost, press 1 or visit FixDirectly.com/signup. This call is completely free."
